# Literature acquisition gaps

The October 7 audit used Stacks on neuroses, institutional repositories, and
publisher/author pages. These full texts remain useful acquisition targets:

- Plischke, Borgonovo and Smith (2013), *Global sensitivity measures from given
  data*, EJOR 226(3), 536–550, DOI 10.1016/j.ejor.2012.11.047. The available
  preview does not establish the legacy Borgonovo test constants' provenance.
- Li, Rosenthal and Rabitz (2001), *High Dimensional Model Representations*,
  J. Phys. Chem. A 105(33), 7765–7777, DOI 10.1021/jp010450t. Metadata and
  abstract checked; full method comparison remains unavailable.
- Tissot and Prieur (2012), *Bias correction for the estimation of sensitivity
  indices based on random balance designs*, DOI 10.1016/j.ress.2012.06.010.
  Abstract/introduction available; full equations were not used in this audit.
- Hickernell (1998), *A generalized discrepancy and quadrature error bound*,
  Math. Comp. 67(221), 299–322, DOI 10.1090/S0025-5718-98-00894-1.
  Bibliographic metadata checked; full-text theorem audit remains outstanding.

Do not substitute a catalog title or a conference version for an inspected
journal text. The lane reports record available versions and recovery paths.
